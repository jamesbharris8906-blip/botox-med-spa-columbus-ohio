Treatments at the spa

Botox

Botox is an FDA-approved shot that relaxes small facial muscles for a short time. It softens forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet while your normal smile and raised brows stay in place. The team places small amounts with care, so the change looks smooth and fresh, never stiff.

Lip Filler

Lip filler uses a soft gel to add shape, balance, and gentle fullness to the lips. The team looks at your lip line, your smile, and the rest of your face before they pick a plan. The goal is lips that look like yours, only a touch more defined and even.

Facial Balancing

Facial balancing looks at the whole face, not one spot at a time. Small amounts of filler go where they can bring the chin, cheeks, lips, and jaw into better balance. The result is a calm, even look that still moves and feels like you.

Microneedling

Microneedling uses very fine needles to make tiny, safe spots on the skin that wake up collagen. Over a few weeks, skin can look smoother and firmer, and small scars and pores can look less clear. It is a good pick when you want slow, steady change.

Cheek Filler

Cheek filler adds soft lift and volume to the middle of the face. It can lift a tired look, shape the cheek line, and help the lower face look less heavy. The team adds it a little at a time, checks the shape in the mirror with you, and stops at a natural point.

Diamond Glow Facial

The Diamond Glow Facial cleans, buffs, and feeds the skin in one visit. A diamond tip lifts dull surface skin, a gentle suction clears pores, and a serum made for your skin goes in next. You walk out with a glow that looks clean and fresh, not shiny or red for long.

Xeomin

Xeomin is an FDA-approved shot much like Botox. It relaxes the same small muscles that make frown lines and forehead lines, and it has no extra proteins in the mix. Some people pick it when they want a clean, simple choice for lines that show when they frown or squint.

Microneedling With Exosomes

This visit pairs microneedling with exosomes, tiny cell signals that help skin heal and grow. The needles open small paths, and the exosomes go to work on tone, texture, and fine lines. Many people pick it when they want more from a microneedling visit.

Hair Restoration Microneedling

Hair restoration microneedling treats the scalp with the same fine-needle method used on the face. It wakes up hair roots and helps the scalp take in helpful serums. It is a slow path, and the team will talk through what to watch for over the first few months.

Chin Filler

Chin filler shapes the chin with a soft gel that adds length, shape, or balance. It can help a weak chin look stronger, smooth a small dip, or make the side view of the face look more even. The team checks the shape from the front and the side before they finish.

Jawline Filler

Jawline filler adds shape along the jaw with a firm, smooth gel. It can make the jaw line look more clear, help a soft jaw look stronger, and bring the lower face into balance with the cheeks and chin. Small amounts, placed with care, give the best look.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Illuminize

The Illuminize Peel is a light, medical-grade peel that lifts dull surface skin with a soft touch. It helps skin look brighter and more even, with little to no flaking for most people. It is a good first peel, or a quick glow before a big day.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Vitalize

The Vitalize Peel goes a bit deeper than Illuminize. It helps with fine lines, uneven tone, and rough texture, and it can leave light peeling for a few days. The team will tell you what to expect and how to care for your skin at home.

SkinMedica Chemical Peels Rejuvanize

The Rejuvanize Peel is the deepest of the three SkinMedica peels here. It works on deeper lines, sun spots, and rough skin, with more peeling and more rest time at home. The team saves it for skin that needs a stronger reset, and they plan the days after with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Botox last?

Most people see results for three to four months. The exact time can shift with your dose, your muscles, and how fast your body clears the product. Your provider can set a repeat date that keeps the soft look going without gaps.

Does Botox hurt?

Most people feel a quick pinch that fades fast. The needle is very fine, and the visit is short. If you are nervous about pain, tell the team first. They can talk you through each step and go at your pace.

How soon will I see results?

Some change can show in three to five days, and the full effect often shows in about two weeks. That slow build is normal. Take a photo on day one and again at two weeks so you can see the change side by side.

Will I look frozen?

Not when the dose and spots fit your face. The team aims for soft movement, not a stiff brow. You should still be able to smile and show feeling. If you want a very light touch, say so at the consult.

What should I avoid after a visit?

Stay upright for a few hours, skip hard workouts that day, and try not to rub the treated spots. Your provider will give you a short list of do's and don'ts. Call the spa at (614) 414-2974 if you have a worry after you leave.

Can I combine Botox with a facial or filler in one visit?

Yes, many people do. A facial can prep the skin, and filler can add shape while Botox softens lines. Your provider will plan the order and the timing so each treatment works well with the others.
